# Photos (website shot list)

Save a photo here named after its slot, for example `ceremony.jpg`. The site picks it up on the
next build and serves resized AVIF/WebP versions. Use the largest original you have (at least
2000 px on the long side) and export as JPEG at high quality.

Only use photos we have a written release for: the couple or host, and the photographer if they
hold the copyright. Never use stock photos of other venues.

**Required** slots block a production build until they exist. Optional slots are hidden in
production until filled. The gallery needs at least 6 real photos.

| Slot | Required | Where it shows | What to shoot |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ceremony` | yes | Home, Weddings, The Farm, Gallery | Ceremony site, late-afternoon light, guests seated |
| `reception` | yes | Weddings, The Farm, Gallery | Reception set for dinner before guests arrive |
| `residence` | yes | Home, The Farm, Gallery | The Residence from outside, warm evening light |
| `celebration` | yes | Home, Events, Gallery | Birthday party: long tables, guests mid-laugh, string lights |
| `gallery-landscape` | no | Gallery | Wide view of the farm and ridge at golden hour (landscape) |
| `couple-field` | no | Home, Gallery | Couple walking through the field at golden hour (portrait) |
| `detail-table` | no | Home, Gallery | Table details: linens, flowers, place cards |
| `evening-lights` | no | Home, Gallery | Reception after dark, lights glowing (portrait) |
| `getting-ready` | no | The Farm, Gallery | Getting-ready room, natural window light |
| `rain-plan` | no | The Farm | The covered or indoor backup space |
| `lawn-games` | no | Gallery | Guests playing lawn games in the afternoon |
| `aerial` | no | The Farm, Gallery | Drone view of the property and the ridge line (landscape) |

The birthday party at the end of October 2026 can cover `celebration`, `detail-table`,
`evening-lights` and `lawn-games`. The two past weddings can cover `ceremony`, `reception` and
`couple-field` once the couples sign releases.
